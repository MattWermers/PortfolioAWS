vision

WhatsForDinner is a web application that quickly finds reciptes well-suited to ingredients users have on hand. It combines user-provided input with required kitchen staples to suggest possible recipes that can be prepared.

motivation

Many people struggle to use up their groceries before they spoil, WhatsForDinner is a web based application that tries to solve the issue. The goal is to save time, money, and reduce food waste.
